1. Introduction

Welcome to LPL ("we," "our," or "us"). LPL is a community football tournament and fantasy league platform that allows users to create and join tournaments, manage teams, and participate in fantasy leagues.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not access the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: When you register for an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored securely via our authentication provider).
  • Profile Information: Profile photo, username, and other details you choose to add to your profile.
  • Tournament & Team Data: Information related to tournaments you create or join, team names, team memberships, rosters, and fantasy league selections.
  • Invite & Communication Data: Information generated when you create or use invite links/codes to join tournaments or teams.
  • Support Communications: Information you provide when contacting us for support or feedback.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the App, including screens viewed, features used, and session duration.
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, and app crash/error reports.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We use Supabase as our backend infrastructure and authentication provider. Supabase processes certain data on our behalf, including authentication credentials and database records, in accordance with their own privacy and security practices.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or abuse
